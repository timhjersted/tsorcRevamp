# Player-Facing Changelog

Curated from pushed `origin/1.4.4-tracked` history plus the 0.17 release-candidate local changes, covering changes from November 2025 through July 9, 2026.

This version removes repeated internal/polish entries, groups related work together, puts the biggest player-facing additions first, and keeps specific balance details where they affect items, weapons, bosses, enemies, or player progression.

## Biggest Additions

- Added a new character class selection menu during new character creation. Players choose between 4 starting classes, each with different starting weapons, light stat adjustments, and Norman's Ring, which gives small class-based stat boosts. The class and new-world menus were also reduced in size to fit smaller resolutions. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added Unkindled Mode as the new default middle difficulty between Classic and Bearer of the Curse. Unkindled gives first-time players access to the mod's Souls-style systems, including Estus, Cerulean Tear, bonfire refills, Souls-mode boss drops, Soul Vessels, Sublime Bone Dust, and related progression, while leaving Bearer of the Curse as the harder opt-in mode. (Tim Hjersted)
- Reworked the Darksign into a tri-state difficulty toggle: Unkindled -> Bearer of the Curse -> Classic -> Unkindled. Existing non-BotC characters migrate to Unkindled on first load; existing BotC characters stay BotC. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added Active Shields, Parries, and a second equipment slot. These systems add new defensive timing and item-use options, with Unkindled weapons now using stamina at half the Bearer of the Curse rate. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added the Boss Hunting Tome, renamed from Boss Rematch Tome, with progression hints and rematch support. The tome now highlights the next critical-path boss, gives era/progression-gated clues for undefeated bosses, and can be opened before any boss has been defeated. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added location names to the map after discovery. Location banners now appear in-game when discovering named areas, and discovered location names appear on the map while zoomed in. (Tim Hjersted)
- Overhauled soapstones. Soapstones are now closed by default, show a "Show {Tag}" prompt, support story/lore/hint/tutorial/location categories, can be filtered in config, and sync JSON edits on world load instead of only on first world creation. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added a new Resource Bar UI for health, mana, and stamina, including yellow delayed-loss feedback and custom stamina display options. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added the Crafting Recipe Guide slot to the player inventory, placed beside the Dark Souls slot. It mirrors Guide recipe functionality and reduces reliance on Recipe Browser. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added new dynamic player hurt and death audio. Hurt sounds scale by damage and remaining health, a Dark Souls retrieval sound was added, new-location discovery got a sound, death now fades to black, and death text now says "YOU DIED". The original Terraria sounds can be restored in config. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added a Dark Souls-inspired Storage tab in the inventory (up to 3000 items) with automatic deposit-on-pickup (potions, boss bags, life/mana crystals, and a few other item types still go to their usual slots), a search bar, and New/All/Weapons/Armor/Accessories/Materials/Misc sorting tabs. Shift-click sends inventory items into Storage and shift-click sends Storage items back to your inventory; the panel remembers whether it was open or closed and can be repositioned by dragging its header. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added an "Only Show Map When Inventory Is Open" toggle (on by default) that hides the minimap and overlay map during normal play, showing it again only while the inventory is open. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added the Oolacile Serpent, a new optional SuperHardMode serpent boss with a long segmented body, ground-following movement, burrow-and-resurface behavior, tail-pierce attacks, venom pressure, and a new treasure bag. (Tim Hjersted)
- Rebuilt Holy Gigas and Ice Gigas into rare boss-style encounters instead of simple legacy enemies. They now have full telegraphed attack kits, poise/stagger interactions, first-kill spell tome rewards, repeat-kill reward chances, and SuperHardMode refights focused on Dark Soul payouts. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added two multi-mode spell tomes, Heart of Winter and Wrath of Gold, based on Ice Gigas and Holy Gigas attacks. Each tome supports left/right click plus tap/hold inputs for four distinct spells. (Tim Hjersted)

## Enemy AI And Combat Feel

- Added the Poise and Stagger system. Opted-in enemies now take modified knockback based on weapon knockback and enemy resistance, build a stagger meter, can be staggered for a short stun, gain temporary poise after being staggered, and receive stunlock protection when pinned against walls. (Tim Hjersted)
- Extended Poise and Stagger support to 46 enemies, with centralized knockback-resistance tuning. Stagger buildup was reduced by 50% as part of the wider rollout. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added enemy hyperarmor during attack windows. Enemies in hyperarmor take no knockback and cannot have their attacks interrupted; stagger bars turn white when staggered during immunity or hyperarmor windows. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added a new enemy kiting behavior. Ranged enemies can now maintain preferred minimum and maximum distance instead of blindly walking into melee range, with looseness tuning so melee players can still close gaps. (Tim Hjersted)
- Expanded enemy evasive profiles. Red Knight-style evasive behavior was generalized into reusable profiles, including Quick Step with iframes, invisibility-on-hit behavior, jump-before-attack options, and profile-based enemy class tuning. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added predator-style invisibility behavior. Assassin and Red Cloud Hunter can go invisible while repositioning and become visible again during telegraphs and attacks. Hunter Potion visibility now helps reveal invisible enemies. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added global self-heal and ally-heal enemy behaviors, including heal telegraphs. Necromancer and Hydris Necromancer received ally-healing support. (Tim Hjersted)
- Upgraded many FighterAI enemies to smarter SF4-style pathfinding with intelligence based on nav radius. Human and intelligent walking enemies can now use more advanced movement, including rope climbing where appropriate. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added patrol/search/pursue behavior to enemy AI so enemies can investigate last-known positions, give up more naturally, and return to patrol states instead of chasing forever. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added better large-enemy movement handling with minimum surface width logic so large sprites avoid odd-looking climbs where most of the sprite floats in the air. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added walking ghost support for wall-passing teleports and ghost afterimages. Grounded ghost enemies with lore-based zero knockback can now be staggered by magic damage or melee weapons infused with magic damage. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added Plague teleport style, including black/purple dust and a plague-smoke attack that applies Curse buildup. Black Knight currently uses this style. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added aggressive teleport behavior for Red Knight and Black Knight after they lose line of sight. (Tim Hjersted)
- Ported Hollow Warrior, Hollow Soldier, Hollow Spearman, Ghost of a Hollow Warrior, Ghost of the Drowned, several Forgotten/Darkmoon ghost enemies, Lothric Knights, Ringed Knight, Tibian Amazon, Tibian Valkyrie, and Basilisk-family enemies into newer AI, stagger, kiting, or evasive systems. (Tim Hjersted)
- Ringed Knight received fire teleport style and a fire teleport attack. (Tim Hjersted)
- SF4 enemies can now teleport out of lava. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added a bomb fuse sound effect and throw sound to enemies' thrown bomb attacks. (Tim Hjersted)
- Improved movement and behavior for large giant/beast enemies such as Gigas, Ice Gigas, Apocalypse, Massacre, and Hydra, including on-hit evasion, kiting, patrol mode, and tile interaction. (Tim Hjersted)
- Hero of Lumelia was converted to the newer SF4 movement AI. (Tim Hjersted)
- Fixed Red Cloud Hunter moving without its walking animation in some cases. (Tim Hjersted)
- SF4 enemies that can't reach a player above them now give up and enter patrol mode instead of jumping endlessly. (Tim Hjersted)
- Reduced the distance of the "big leap back" evasion behavior so it looks less exaggerated. (Tim Hjersted)
- Added an SF4 "Flee" state: enemies that genuinely can't reach the player while being hit will run away up to 40 tiles before returning to patrol/wander, unless they can teleport. (Tim Hjersted)
- Raised the SF4 navigation jump-power cap so strong-jumping enemies aren't capped at 8 tiles (up to 30 tiles is now possible); Ghost of a Forgotten Knight jumps higher as a result. (Tim Hjersted)
- Jungle Wyvern Juveniles now only spawn in the jungle and dungeon during Pre-Hardmode (previously also spawned in the Earth Temple after Skeletron defeat); Forgotten Knight's graveyard spawns now also require Skeletron to be defeated. (Tim Hjersted)
- Fixed ghost enemies that pass through walls getting stuck directly above or below the player. (Tim Hjersted)
- Tuned SF4 navigation search radius, kiting looseness, and minimum range for several enemies. (Tim Hjersted)

## Player Progression And Systems

- Standing near a bonfire now allows crafting with Dark Souls, while Demon Altar crafting remains available in the field. (Tim Hjersted)
- Dropped souls are no longer lost when saving and reloading. (Tim Hjersted)
- Newly created adventure and classic worlds now update their date-created stat to the current date, making multiple playthroughs easier to track. (Tim Hjersted)
