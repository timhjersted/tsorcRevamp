## Invaders

Invader banner tiers are independent of the boss roster and `NPC.boss`:

- Khaios: Great Invader 
- Cursed Dragon: Great Invader
- All other puppet invasions: Invader

This is an intentionally easy-to-revise default. Individual invaders can override their tier
without changing Terraria's mechanical boss flag.
